Here’s a text-based wireframe flow to visualize the MVP screens. Think of each screen as a simple sketch layout showing what’s on it and how the user moves forward.

---

## **MVP User Flow Wireframes**

### **1. Onboarding / Home Screen**

* **Top:** Logo + Welcome message
* **Middle:** Quick actions → [Start Weekly Plan], [View Store Deals], [Saved Recipes]
* **Bottom Nav:** Home | Explore | Shopping List | Profile

---

### **2. Budget Input Screen**

* **Top:** "Set Your Weekly Budget"
* **Middle:**

  * Input field: $ [   150   ]
  * Options: [Number of people] [Dietary Preferences]
  * Time-to-cook slider (Quick meals ↔ Long prep)
* **CTA Button:** → Generate Plan

---

### **3. Store Deals Map / List Screen**

* **Top Tabs:** [Map View] | [List View]
* **Map:** Pins showing nearby stores

  * Click a pin → popup with deals summary (e.g., “Chicken breast $1.99/lb”)
* **List:** Scrollable deals by store
* **CTA Button:** → Continue to Meal Plan

---

### **4. Recipe Exploration Screen**

* **Top:** "Choose How to Explore Recipes"
* **Tabs:** [New Recipes] | [Healthiest Recipes] | [Most Popular]
* **Cards (3 per row):** Recipe name + thumbnail + key ingredients on sale
* **Tap Recipe:** Opens mini iFrame of external recipe OR internal recipe card
* **CTA:** → Add to Meal Plan

---

### **5. Meal Plan Summary Screen**

* **Top:** “Your Weekly Plan”
* **Middle:** Calendar grid Mon–Sun with breakfast/lunch/dinner slots

  * Each slot: small recipe image + title
  * Long press: option to swap or remove recipe
* **CTA Button:** → Generate Shopping List

---

### **6. Shopping List Screen**

* **Top Tabs:** [By Recipe] | [By Store]
* **List View:** Checkboxes for each ingredient

  * Ingredient + best store + sale price
* **CTA Buttons:** [Export to PDF] [Export to Instacart]

---

### **7. Profile / Settings (Basic for MVP)**

* **Options:**

  * Edit budget + preferences
  * Household accounts (add/remove)
  * Saved meal plans

---

## **Flow Summary**

1. Home → Budget Input → Store Deals → Recipe Exploration (3 options) → Meal Plan → Shopping List → Export.
2. Bottom nav allows switching between Home, Explore (recipes), Shopping List, and Profile at any time.
